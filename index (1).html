Get your support ticket solved faster

For anyone reporting a problem with a new system. Written from the support side of 200+ go-live requests.

Knowledge baseEnd users

Before you call or submit a ticket

Most delays happen because the support team has to come back and ask questions. Gather these five details first and your issue can often be fixed on the first contact.

  1. Say what you were trying to do. "Place a lab order for a patient" tells us far more than "the system isn't working."
  2. Note exactly what happened. Copy the error message word for word, or take a screenshot. Hide any patient or client details first.
  3. Check if it happens again. Try once more. Does it happen every time, or only sometimes? Only for you, or for colleagues too?
  4. Share where and when. Your location or department, the device you used, and the time it happened.
  5. Tell us how urgent it is. Is anyone blocked from working, or is there a workaround for now?

What a great ticket looks like

Trying to: print a discharge summary What happened: "Unable to print: no default printer" (screenshot attached) Happens: every time, 2 colleagues see it too Where / when: 3rd floor unit, workstation 12, since 9:40 am Urgency: blocking discharges, no workaround yet

Why this works: the support team can reproduce the problem, route it to the right team the first time, and skip the back-and-forth.
